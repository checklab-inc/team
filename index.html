障害者雇用、放置していませんか？Is Your Disability Employment Obligation Being Left Unaddressed?

法定雇用率Statutory Employment Rate

2.7%の雇用義務2.7% Employment Obligation

2026年7月1日より、法定雇用率は2.5%から2.7%へ引き上げられました。対象となる企業規模も常時雇用労働者数「40.0人以上」から「37.5人以上」へ拡大し、義務の対象企業が増えています。As of July 1, 2026, the statutory employment rate was raised from 2.5% to 2.7%. The scope of applicable companies has also expanded, from those with 40.0 or more regular employees to those with 37.5 or more, increasing the number of companies subject to this obligation.

未達成の場合If the Quota Is Not Met

納付金 月5万円／人Levy: JPY 50,000/month per person

法定雇用障害者数に不足する人数に応じて、1人につき月額50,000円の障害者雇用納付金の納付義務が生じます。Companies falling short of the statutory number of employees with disabilities are required to pay a disability employment levy of JPY 50,000 per month for each person short of the quota.

改善しない場合If Not Improved

最終的に企業名を公表Company Name Publicly Disclosed

企業訪問・計画書提出・呼出指導・局長勧告と段階が進み、最終的には企業名公表というペナルティに至ります。The process escalates through company visits, submission of improvement plans, summoned guidance, and director recommendations, ultimately resulting in the penalty of public disclosure of the company's name.
